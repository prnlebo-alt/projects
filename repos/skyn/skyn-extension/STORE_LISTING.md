Full description:
Skyn lets you apply visual skins to your favorite AI assistants.

Tired of staring at the same default interface every day? Skyn gives Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini a look that fits your taste.

Features:

- Works on Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini
- 120 skins across dark, light, vibrant, glass, holiday, sports-inspired, abstract, attitude, pretty, nature, sci-fi, gaming, and special styles
- 3 free skins included
- Custom background image upload
- Font customization
- Skin marketplace for premium styles and packs

Privacy:

Skyn is CSS-only on supported AI sites. It does not read, collect, transmit, or store your conversations. It does not inspect prompts, responses, cookies, or site storage.

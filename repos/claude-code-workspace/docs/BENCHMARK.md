# Benchmark and Acceptance Gates — v1.0

The August 23 competitive report is a methodological reference, not the strategic answer key.

## Test A — Process Fidelity

Create/freeze a human-labeled corpus sampled from verified source material. Labels: factual accuracy, evidence class, interest flags, maturity, independent-vs-syndicated family, grounding validity, contradiction status, materiality.

Phase 1/2 gates:

1. 100% of `verified` claims satisfy grounding/provenance state-machine contract; materiality recall >=95% on labeled corpus.
2. Zero fabricated URLs/source identifiers.
3. >=95% correct vendor-reported/self-interested labeling.
4. >=90% maturity taxonomy agreement.
5. >=90% syndicated-vs-independent family accuracy.
6. 100% deterministic material verification selection; audit sample reproducible.
7. Every benchmark score dimension has queryable provenance once scoring exists.
8. Unsourced Strategic Fit/Competitive Advantage cannot generate high-confidence BUILD.

## Test B — Held-Out Generalization

Before Phase 4 completes, evaluate against a bounded market not covered by the baseline report, or a genuinely later evidence set. Default: agentic NetSuite/ERP finance automation for PE-backed mid-market companies. Blind evaluator grades reasoning process, provenance, maturity discipline, contested-lane avoidance, and counter-evidence handling.

## Phase 3 gates

9. Seeded material change recall >=95%.
10. False-positive HIGH/CRITICAL change-event rate <=5%.
11. Competitor velocity reproducible and no bucket-boundary discontinuity.

## Phase 4/5 gates

12. PARTNER preemption fires on labeled mature-specialist scenarios.
13. BUILD exception cannot pass without elevated advantage, evidence, Gauntlet, and human approval.
14. Held-out generalization receives blind PASS.
15. Gauntlet names at least one weakness tied to a concrete kill condition or material score input; cosmetic caveats do not count.

No UI-first work if blocking intelligence gates fail.
